Read the full biography

Mfehe-Abasi Ekpe is an indigene of Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria, a graduate of Philosophy, market analyst, entrepreneur, leader, motivational speaker, singer and business consultant. He is the CEO of Excel Pips Academy and the Director of The Synergy Leadership Academy, two platforms through which he pursues financial education, personal development, leadership and the empowerment of individuals. He is also learning graphic design to broaden his creative skills.

As a philosopher, he believes in critical thinking, rational inquiry and continuous learning, and that personal growth begins with the willingness to question, learn, unlearn and improve. For him, true leadership is shown through service, responsibility, influence and the ability to inspire others.

As President of the National Association of Philosophy Students (NAPS), Akwa Ibom State University Chapter, he received the Most Outstanding Departmental President of the Year Award from the Students' Union Government, a milestone that reflected his commitment to student leadership, service and organisational development.

Through The Synergy Leadership Academy, he envisions a community where people from different backgrounds share their stories, knowledge, cultures and achievements, and learn from one another across borders. His broader vision is to equip people for personal growth, financial independence, purposeful living and lasting impact.

    Who can join the academy?

    Anyone who shares the vision: students, graduates, professionals, entrepreneurs, researchers, creatives and anyone passionate about leadership and personal development. People who have experienced what The Synergy stands for are especially welcome.

    Do I need experience?

    No. You don't need extensive experience. What matters most is your willingness to contribute, learn from others and work toward shared goals.

    What if I'm interested in more than one department?

    Choose the one you'd most like to serve in, then mention the others in the "Anything else?" box on the form. It goes into your message automatically.

    How do I send my registration?

    Fill in the form, choose WhatsApp or email, and tap Send. Your app opens with every detail already written. Press Send there to finish. You can also send your details yourself to 0907 760 1423 or thesynergyleadershipacademy@gmail.com.

    Can organisations partner with the academy?

    Yes. The academy welcomes partners and supporters. For partnerships, contracts and collaborations, message the Director directly on WhatsApp.
